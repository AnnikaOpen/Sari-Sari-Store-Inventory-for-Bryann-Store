WHERE EACH OOP CONCEPT IS USED (for the defense)

ENCAPSULATION  - Product, Person, SaleItem: private fields + getters/setters.
INHERITANCE    - Staff extends Person; Customer extends Person;
                 StaffDAO / CustomerDAO extend BaseDAO.
ABSTRACTION    - Person (abstract class, abstract getRole()); BaseDAO (abstract class);
                 Crud<T> and Refreshable (interfaces).
POLYMORPHISM   - getRole() and toString() behave differently in Staff and Customer;
                 PersonPanel works with Person and shows both Staff and Customers;
                 MainFrame reloads any tab through the Refreshable interface.

DATABASE (MySQL via JDBC): DBConnection, DataStore (PRODUCT + login), StaffDAO, CustomerDAO, SaleDAO.
SaleDAO.checkout() uses a transaction: saves SALE + SALE_ITEM and lowers PRODUCT stock together.

PROCESS FLOW: Login > Products (manage stock) > New Sale (cart, customer, payment, checkout)
              > Stock goes down > Sales History (report)
